VOCAB RIDER — OPINION ARENA (OFFLINE EDITION)
=============================================

HOW TO PLAY
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html — the game opens in your browser right away.
3. No internet, no install, no account. Works anywhere.

TIP: keep the assets folder next to index.html — it holds
all the neon sticker art the game uses.
TIP: use a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
On a phone or tablet, open the file with your browser
(choose 'Open with Chrome').

WHAT'S INSIDE
- ONE unit: OPINION ARENA — 10 arenas from BANGKOK to NIGHT CITY
- 30 debate words: opinion, agree, disagree, because, reason,
  evidence, persuade, conclusion and more
- 10 arcade mini-games, including NEON EVIDENCE (match a photo to
  its word), STICKER STORM (name the sticker) and DEBATE DUEL
  (choose the best opinion reply)
- JET the cyber-cat co-pilot with hints for sentence order and anagrams
- Avatar system, 910 points and 30 stars
- A printable certificate with a full city-by-city report (print or PDF)

Score and progress are saved in the browser on that device
(localStorage), so you can close the game and continue later.
